Pi Pocket is installed

Open Pi Pocket from Spotlight, Launchpad, or the Applications folder of your home folder. It opens a Terminal window: choose how your devices reach Pi Pocket, then open the sign-in link on your phone. Press q in that window, or close it, to stop Pi Pocket.

Menu → Update Pi Pocket updates it. Pi's tools work best with git: if you do not have it, run xcode-select --install in Terminal.

To uninstall, run this in Terminal:

"$HOME/Library/Application Support/Pi Pocket/uninstall.sh"
