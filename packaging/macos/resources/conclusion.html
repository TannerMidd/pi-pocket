Pi Pocket is installed

Open Pi Pocket from Spotlight, Launchpad, or the Applications folder of your home folder. It opens in a window of its own, signed in, and puts π in the menu bar. It listens on this Mac only. To sign in your phone, use Menu → Sign in another device. To stop Pi Pocket, choose Quit Pi Pocket.

Pi Pocket (console) opens the launcher in Terminal instead: there you choose how other devices reach Pi Pocket (the local network, a Cloudflare tunnel, or Tailscale), and it shows the sign-in QR code.

Menu → Update Pi Pocket updates it. Pi's tools work best with git: if you do not have it, run xcode-select --install in Terminal.

To uninstall, run this in Terminal:

"$HOME/Library/Application Support/Pi Pocket/uninstall.sh"
